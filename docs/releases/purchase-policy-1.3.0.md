# Clearer purchase and return policies — prepared

The policy revision separates optional store exchanges from applicable consumer withdrawal rights. It explains who pays shipping in each case, keeps discounted-product restrictions within voluntary benefits, and gives customers explicit choices when a product cannot be supplied or a price discrepancy needs resolution.

A compact contents menu connects fulfillment, returns, shipping, marketplace purchases and gift terms. Marketplace seller rules link back to the relevant shared section, while settings-driven commercial figures remain in their existing owner. Updated controls use the storefront's shared colors and inherited typeface.

Validation includes 21 policy checks and eight combined storefront/marketplace browser previews across mobile/desktop and light/dark themes. Interactive checks cover anchors, keyboard focus, hover, touch targets and horizontal overflow; screenshots were inspected. The companion marketplace flow suite passes 335 checks. The browser previews do not alter the live pages.

The revision is prepared, not installed. Production activation and business-specific legal review remain pending. The copy does not promise immunity from consumer claims or override mandatory rights.
