# Clearer purchase and return policies — installed

The policy revision separates optional store exchanges from applicable consumer withdrawal rights. It explains who pays shipping in each case, keeps discounted-product restrictions within voluntary benefits, and gives customers explicit choices when a product cannot be supplied or a price discrepancy needs resolution.

Outdated benefit badges are replaced with text links consistent with the current terms. A compact contents menu connects fulfillment, returns, shipping, marketplace purchases and gift terms. Marketplace seller rules link back to the relevant shared section, while settings-driven commercial figures remain in their existing owner. Updated controls use the storefront's shared colors and inherited typeface.

Validation includes 22 policy checks and eight combined storefront/marketplace browser previews across mobile/desktop and light/dark themes. Interactive checks cover anchors, keyboard focus, hover, touch targets and horizontal overflow; screenshots were inspected. The companion marketplace flow suite passes 335 checks. The browser previews do not alter the live pages.

The revision is installed with owner approval. Scoped backups and post-install source checks are complete. A further 500 read-only checks cover the eight installed page configurations, including reciprocal navigation and the versioned stylesheet. Offline/PWA behavior is outside this validation. Business-specific legal review remains recommended. The copy does not promise immunity from consumer claims or override mandatory rights.
