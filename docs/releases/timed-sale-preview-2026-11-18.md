# Timed sale preview and brand discovery

A compact announcement above the store navigation now counts down to a scheduled sale. At the start, its wording switches to time remaining in the event; after the scheduled end, the announcement and temporary home sections disappear automatically. Store staff can edit the campaign title, dates and optional destination from an admin screen.

The home page adds a short campaign introduction and a scrollable, logo-led rail of brands with available discounted products. The rail is derived from current catalogue data, so it does not imply that a brand has an offer when none is available. Existing product prices, stock and discount rules remain the source of truth.

The approved release was installed with a recoverable backup. Local mobile and desktop checks passed in light and dark themes, including countdown transitions, brand scrolling, keyboard focus, text contrast and horizontal overflow. The installed files, loaded settings, generated brand cards and public assets were checked after release. A full live browser visual review remains pending because the storefront restricts access from the current test location. No sales or conversion improvement is claimed.
