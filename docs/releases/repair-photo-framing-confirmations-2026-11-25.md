# Deliberate watch decisions and a clearer photo review

Repair staff now see a separate confirmation before approving a watch, rejecting it or sending a counter-price. The confirmation identifies the selected watch and amount. Cancel or Escape leaves the review unchanged, and repeated confirmation sends one action.

The shared photo editor offers rotation, size and reset controls, keeps the whole watch centered on white and keeps the save-and-replace action visible while scrolling. Closing the editor leaves its case open. Staff session renewal is shared with the editor, with no automatic repeat after uncertain network or server outcomes.

Publication remains conditional on reviewed identity, agreed price and the current case state. Authorized managers can publish ready cases; missing documents and pending identity review have separate explanations. This does not widen access to seller identity documents.

## Independent identity review and publication controls

Repair 2.17.11 separates identity review from watch approval. Allocated marketplace reviewers can inspect submitted evidence inside waiting, returned and approved cases, approve valid evidence or reject it with a reason. The document viewer closes after the decision, and old document access is denied. Incomplete evidence can be rejected but cannot be approved.

Publication has its own control and a specific explanation when identity, price, photographs or listing state prevent publication. Both controls refresh the same case after a decision. A final confirmation remains required before publishing. Existing app allocation boundaries remain in place.
