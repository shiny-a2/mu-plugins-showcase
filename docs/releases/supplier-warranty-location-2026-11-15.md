# Automatic supplier locations — 15 November 2026

Eligible, available supplier-stock items now match their warranty provider to the
existing company list. Counted shop stock, recorded allocations, explicit staff
assignments and separate delivery policies retain their existing behavior.
Missing or ambiguous company names are shown for review instead of guessed.

The operations form, order columns, export and internal order cards share the
result. Staff can retain automatic reporting or select a company explicitly;
the card explains when its company came from warranty information.

Validation covers supplier eligibility, spelling variants, multiple providers,
manual overrides and branch preservation using synthetic data. Producer-to-bot
contract checks and real form interactions pass at mobile and desktop widths.
Production verification checks exact deployed sources, read-only contracts and
the running bot task; no fabricated order or test group message is used.
