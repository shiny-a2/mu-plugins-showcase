# Competitor brand evidence — released

Released 18 November 2026 in the paired competitor report and collector updates. The first activation was restored after a source-label contract error; a revised, tested release was then activated. The report now distinguishes the number of unique watch references observed at a retailer from possible stock-change signals, and allows managers to drill down by retailer, brand, reference and dated price observation. Brands absent from the store's catalogue are marked separately.

A new public Banimode watch source contributed an initial observation of 16,200 normalized models across 96 brands. Its category coverage is incomplete and is labelled partial in the report. The first observation produced no possible sales signals because there is no previous day for comparison. A retailer's stock disappearance is not a verified order.

Where a public listing reports quantity, the reference history retains that quantity without treating it as a sales or inventory-value fact. A price before discount appears only when the published base price, final price and discount agree. Replaced listing identifiers and simultaneous whole-brand withdrawals are held for review instead of becoming possible sales signals. Older observations remain readable.

The revised release passed source-contract, collector and report tests, as well as interactive mobile and desktop checks in light and dark themes. Live report and reference readbacks confirmed the partial source label, brand drilldown, quantity and corroborated list price. The first uncached aggregate read was slow; a later cached read was fast. Authenticated browser acceptance and another daily observation remain to be checked. This release does not establish competitor order counts or profit.
