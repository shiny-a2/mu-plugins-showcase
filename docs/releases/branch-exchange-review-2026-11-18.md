# One purchase after a counter exchange

An accounting export can list a replacement watch at its full price under a second invoice number. Counting both rows as sales can overstate the customer's purchases and rewards. The prepared update connects an explicitly referenced exchange to its original purchase. The branch manager can review the change in price, items, stock and loyalty points before applying it. Missing product or stock evidence keeps the request open for investigation.

Tests with synthetic workbooks exercised new and previously recorded exchanges, repeated uploads, spent rewards and recovery from an interrupted update. Customer and staff screens were checked at mobile and desktop widths.
