# Clearer customer invoices and club launch controls

The customer purchase list used an internal record ID as its invoice number and implied that every displayed purchase earned rewards. The installed update shows the accounting reference and fiscal year, labels returns and purchases that do not earn points, and explains when an amount needs reconciliation. A confirmed accounting duplicate stays in the accounting records without appearing as another customer purchase.

Linked exchange documents remain attached to their original purchase without becoming another customer purchase. The list is restricted to the signed-in customer. A storage error gives a visible retry message. Existing account navigation and the inherited typeface are preserved, with shared light and dark theme colors and wrapping for long item names.

The launch messaging screen also separates the selected audience from all previously accepted introductions. An empty selected audience no longer implies that every customer received a message. Provider acceptance is described separately from receipt on a phone. Accounting holds and uncertain delivery results remain subject to review.

Validation covered 27 invoice-consumer checks against actual SQL and the established reward policy, 68 messaging regressions with intercepted transport, and 60 browser assertions at mobile and desktop widths in both themes. Browser checks exercised tab navigation, keyboard focus, review and failure states, return-amount direction, contrast and horizontal overflow. Read-only inspection checked compatibility with the installed runtime. The template guide records the approved copy and token contracts.

The two-file runtime update was installed after protected backups and source checks. A fresh native runtime verified accounting references and fiscal years, rejected foreign and anonymous history reads before the invoice query, and confirmed the saved campaign selection. These checks do not establish authenticated customer acceptance or campaign delivery. No real customer message or financial record was created for testing, and this display package does not change invoice amounts, reward balances or wallet entries. Financial source reconciliation was a separate approved release with its own verification.
