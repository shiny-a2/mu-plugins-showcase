# Clearer repair and marketplace notifications — prepared

A completed watch registration and a published listing should reach the staff responsible for that marketplace. A request that moved quickly through review could miss its first notification, and a branch restriction could hide notices from the assigned technician.

The prepared update connects completed registrations and publication stages to the authorized repair inbox. It preserves separate branch counters, existing read history and explicit app access. Repeated collection keeps the same notice, while a permitted transfer remains visible to its responsible staff.

Device settings distinguish a registered browser from an unsuccessful delivery attempt. Explicit activation can renew a stale subscription, and a self-test checks transport without clearing unread work. Messages shown outside the app remain generic.

Validation covered collection, access denial, duplicate prevention, failure recovery, pagination and read counts. The actual publication event was checked across the marketplace and repair modules. Mobile and desktop interactions passed in both themes, including retry feedback, keyboard focus, contrast, navigation and explicit subscription renewal.

This is a prepared release. Installation and physical-device delivery remain unverified. No real customer record, publication or notification was created for testing.
