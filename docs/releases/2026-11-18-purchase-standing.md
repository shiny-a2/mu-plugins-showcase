# Immediate purchase rewards and staff receipts

A confirmed website payment or a qualifying counter invoice can now make points available as soon as the purchase is recorded. Staff can see a customer's tier, point value and wallet balance on the sale receipt. A purchase that appears in both channels counts once, and previous pending rewards keep their original release dates.

A split payment shows individual amounts when they reconcile with the invoice total. Receipts identify an incomplete allocation so staff can follow it up. Tests in a disposable commerce environment covered re-imports, repeated payment callbacks, a partial refund, cancellation, paired sales and access to receipt information.
