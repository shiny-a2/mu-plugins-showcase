# Repair workspace polish 2.15.1

The repair app now leads staff through customer lookup before choosing a service. Forms, records, notices and action dialogs share a clearer visual hierarchy across phone and desktop layouts, in light and dark themes. The customer invoice view is easier to scan and use on a phone.

The separate final-invoice action is available. If the messaging provider has not approved its dedicated template, staff see that delivery failed and can retry after approval. A recorded invoice is not presented as a delivered message. Existing review controls still require staff to confirm a payment before the repair balance is settled.

Synthetic finance and interaction checks covered invoice retry, access boundaries, duplicate settlement prevention and responsive screen states. The deployed source matched the reviewed release, and the public app shell and assets responded successfully. Real message delivery and authenticated production actions were not exercised for presentation testing.

Released on 11 November 2026 with a recoverable source backup.
