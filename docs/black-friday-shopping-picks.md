# Two ways to shop the sale

The campaign landing page gives shoppers a starting point for two different budgets: a low-priced watch and one with a large percentage saving. Each card links to the corresponding watch and displays its product image, regular price and current price. If both selections identify the same watch, the page shows one card.

Candidate selection uses the store catalog. The page checks stock, visibility, the active discount and the product image before rendering. The landing page was checked on phones and desktops in both themes, including keyboard focus, card links and image loading.

When a sale price, relevant availability or publication status changes, the campaign clears the ranked candidates and its cached landing page at the end of the request. Multiple updates in one request share a single refresh. Campaign tests cover the grouped refresh and its page scope.

## Shipping and the end of the campaign

During the sale, eligible postal destinations offer free standard post and paid express post. Selecting standard post explains the dispatch delay before the shopper confirms the order.

The countdown and free shipping end together at midnight Tehran time after 10 Azar. The home page then returns to its regular banners. WooCommerce schedules discounted products to return to their regular prices at the same time. Tests checked simple product and variation prices before and after the deadline, including the interval before the scheduled worker completes.

The storefront uses the campaign name **بلک فرایدی جواهریان** in the countdown, home and landing headings, and banner captions. The landing page title also appears in search and social sharing metadata. The countdown’s accessible label includes the campaign name.

### Shop a watch from the banner

The home and sale banners rotate through seven watch references. Each offer pairs a cutout from that model’s catalog photograph with its current WooCommerce price, percentage discount and add-to-cart button. Shoppers can pause the rotation or advance to another watch. Reduced-motion preferences keep automatic playback off by default.

The brand rail includes every eligible brand with a visible, in-stock sale product. Desktop and mobile checks covered the correspondence between each photograph, product link and cart form, as well as image loading and manual navigation in both themes.

### A larger rotation for the home page

The campaign now has a pool of twelve women’s references and twelve men’s references from seven brands. It alternates between the two groups while both have eligible watches. Only the first photograph loads initially. The first visit to another slide loads that watch’s WebP. The seven original WebP files together went from 190,324 to 136,194 bytes.

The home banner combines the watch offer, cart action and link to the sale page in one place. Visitors can go back, advance or pause and see their position in the rotation. Browser checks exercised all 24 offers on the home and campaign layouts at three widths in light and dark mode.

The Tissot PRX ice-blue reference now shows the full bracelet in its carousel photograph. The revised image has its own cache version so returning shoppers receive the corrected cutout.

### Velvet carousel and ranked offers

The home carousel places each watch against burgundy velvet and opens with the largest discount in its available photo selection. Shoppers can swipe or use the arrow keys to compare models. On a change, the watch photographs briefly crossfade while the price and cart action switch together. A separate rail displays high-discount watches from the wider catalog, with their product photographs and prices.

Browser checks cycled through the 24 reference photographs at four widths in both themes. The checks also covered reduced motion, rapid navigation and the link between each displayed model and its cart action.

The mobile layout keeps the watch, price and purchase button on the same fabric. Autoplay advances every three seconds while the carousel is visible and idle. Alongside percentage discounts, shoppers can browse two randomized collections: savings above ten million toman and sale prices between twenty and fifty million toman. Each collection opens at its matching section on the sale landing.
