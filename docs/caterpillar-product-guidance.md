# Evidence-led Caterpillar product guidance

Twenty available Caterpillar product pages were reviewed in current search-demand order. Sixteen exact references now have individual Persian buying guidance, concise summaries, factual search descriptions and narrowly corrected catalogue attributes supported by manufacturer material. Four references remain unchanged because exact primary evidence was not available; a nearby model or retailer description was deliberately not treated as proof.

The release removes unsupported representation, authenticity, warranty and manufacturing-origin wording from the owned copy without changing product titles, URLs, prices, stock, images or categories. This keeps catalogue guidance useful without turning assumptions into product facts.

Publication used exact-reference, content-hash and attribute preflight guards with a private recovery snapshot. Independent application-data and live-HTML readback confirmed the intended sixteen changes, four untouched holds, preserved commerce and unrelated metadata, indexable canonicals, Product/InStock offers, sitemap coverage and repeated cache hits.

Sixty-four live mobile/desktop light/dark interaction states passed keyboard, focus, overflow, contrast and structured-data checks. No ranking, AI citation, conversion or sales improvement is claimed before a later measurement window.
